# Viper's Gambit: lore (approved; lore text lives in Lore.lua, abilities in Powers.lua)

This document is the approved lore. Lore text lives in Lore.lua and abilities in Powers.lua (one unique ability per snake).

## The world: Serpentia
Long ago the **First Serpent** shed its skin across the world. Each scale landed somewhere different and grew into a **Den**,
and every snake alive descends from one of them:

| Lane type | Den | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Land | **The Dustwild**: dry plains, burrows, ruins | tough, plain, stubborn |
| Water | **The Drowned Reaches**: rivers, marshes, reefs | slippery, patient, poisonous |
| Tree | **The Canopy**: vines, reeds, jungle | ambushers, constrictors |
| Spirit | **The Hollow**: where snakes go when they die | undead, shadow, fire |
| Rainbow | **Wildborn**: snakes that belong to no Den | bright, rare, fit any lane |

Lanes accepting two types each is the Dens' old **Border Truces**: every Den shares two borders, which is why a Land snake
can hold a Water lane but never a Spirit one.

## Venom and the Gambit
**Venom** is the First Serpent's blood, still seeping into the world. It powers every snake and is how every duel is paid for.
A **Gambit** is a formal duel where each side wagers its **fangs** (health). You do not fight with your own hands: you are a
**Charmer**, and your snakes answer to your venom. Lose all your fangs and you owe the Court a favour.

## The Court and the ladder
The **Viper's Court** rules Serpentia and holds a Gambit ladder of ten rungs. Beat all ten to challenge the **Viper King** for
the **Crown Fang**. There are many **Charmers** throughout the lands, and you are just one of them, climbing for a reason of your own.

| # | Opponent | Den | One-line story |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden Keeper | Dustwild | Tends the Court's walled garden and tests every newcomer with harmless garter snakes. |
| 2 | Pond Skulker | Drowned Reaches | Hides in the lily pads and strikes only when you are distracted. |
| 3 | Venom Rookie | Mixed | A young Charmer who stole a venom vial and wants to prove it was not luck. |
| 4 | Crypt Warden | Hollow | Guards the Court's tombs and thinks the dead make the best soldiers. |
| 5 | Reed Stalker | Canopy | Lives among the reeds and never fights a duel in the open. |
| 6 | Fang Brawler | Dustwild | Fights for fun; keeps every snake that has ever bitten him. |
| 7 | Constrictor | Canopy | Slow, patient, and has never lost a duel by turn count alone. |
| 8 | Ember Queen | Hollow | Rules the lava vents under the Hollow and keeps her fire snakes close. |
| 9 | Golden Heir | Wildborn | The King's spoiled heir, dripping in gold snakes he did not earn. |
| 10 | The Viper King | All Dens | Wears the Crown Fang, sleeps in a coil of Cobras, and has not been challenged in a century. |

The five **Cobras** (Cobra, King, Albino, Blood, Frost) and the Golden Snake are the Court's **royal line** and cannot be found in packs;
only the King's family wields them (which is why they are unobtainable except by an unlock).

## Tiers
Tier follows rarity (1 to 5) and is shown on the info page. More Tier 4 snakes and the five Tier 5 legendaries are coming, so this lore gets updated when they are added.

| Rarity | Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hatchling** | everyday snakes, no abilities |
| 2 | **Fanged** | working snakes, simple abilities |
| 3 | **Ancient** | old and dangerous, strong abilities |
| 4 | **Royal** | the King's line |
| 5 | **Mythic** | the five legendaries (added by the other chat; lore to be updated once they land) |

## Abilities
Every snake above Tier 1 gets its own unique ability (Tier 1 snakes get small ones too), and stronger, costlier snakes get stronger ones. The full list is in `Powers.lua`; the text shown in game is the ability's `text`.

## Snake lore (one or two lines each) with proposed ability

### Land (the Dustwild)
- **Snake**: The plain, original snake, the one every Charmer starts with.
- **Big_Snake**: Grew too large for its burrow and has been blocking the road since.
- **Baby_Snake**: Hatched yesterday and bites anything that moves.
- **Well_Fed_Snake**: Sleeps after meals, so it never needs to attack.
- **Cannibal_Snake**: Eats other snakes; the Court bans it from banquets.
- **Cobra**: The Court's guard animal, hood flared to show the way out.
- **King_Cobra**: Rules the Dustwild dens and expects bows.
- **Albino_Cobra**: Bleached by moonlight; considered a bad omen at court.
- **Horned_Viper**: Its horns are a warning; nobody has ignored them twice.
- **Grey_Snake**: Blends into the dust so well it is rarely counted.
- **Dirt_Snake**: Hides under a handful of soil and waits.
- **Sand_Snake**: Moves like a ripple through dunes.

### Water (the Drowned Reaches)
- **Anaconda**: Rules the deepest river and swallows what disagrees.
- **Extra_Long_Python**: Measured once, then the tape ran out.
- **Frost_Cobra**: A royal cobra frozen mid-hiss in a glacier, now thawed and cross.
- **Sea_Snake**: The reef's quiet guard; its venom is stronger than it looks.
- **Toxic_Snake**: Bred in poisoned ponds; touching it is a mistake.
- **Mud_Snake**: Hard to hold and harder to see.
- **Ball_Python**: Curls up in a ball and waits for the danger to go away.
- **Garter_Snake**: Harmless, friendly, and the first lesson in every Gambit.
- **Copperhead**: Strikes from leaf litter and vanishes.

### Tree (the Canopy)
- **Boa_Constrictor**: The Canopy's strongest squeeze; trees creak around it.
- **Tree_Boa**: Hangs from branches and drops without warning.
- **Vine_Snake**: Looks like a vine until it moves.
- **Grass_Snake**: Always found, never caught.
- **Worm_Snake**: Tiny and shy, often mistaken for dinner.
- **Black_Mamba**: Fastest of the Canopy; its bite is a short story.
- **Kingsnake**: Eats other snakes and wears the stripes of a diplomat.
- **Corn_Snake**: Farmers' friend and the Charmer's favourite first pet.
- **Brown_Snake**: Seen everywhere, remembered nowhere.

### Spirit (the Hollow)
- **Zombie_Snake**: Died, got up again, and has been angry since.
- **Skeleton_Snake**: Only bones now, which makes it very hard to squeeze.
- **Ghost_Snake**: Passes through walls, locked doors, and defences.
- **Shadow_Snake**: A snake-shaped gap in the light.
- **Lava_Snake**: Swims in magma and leaves molten tracks.
- **Blood_Cobra**: The Court's executioner; its hood is the colour of its work.
- **Pale_Snake**: What a snake becomes when everything has already happened.

### Wildborn (any lane)
- **Coral_Snake**: Red, yellow, black: a warning wrapped in a rhyme.
- **Rattlesnake**: Announces itself and still wins.
- **Gaboon_Viper**: Fangs longer than a thumb and a temper to match.
- **Golden_Snake**: Said to be a gift from the First Serpent; the Golden Heir's pride.
